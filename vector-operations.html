What are Vectors?

Vectors are mathematical objects that have both magnitude (size) and direction. Unlike regular numbers (called scalars), vectors tell us not just how much, but also which way.

Real-world examples:

  • Force: Pushing a box with 10 Newtons of force to the right
  • Velocity: A car moving at 60 km/h north
  • Displacement: Walking 5 meters east

Vector notation: We represent vectors with arrows (→) or bold letters. In this lesson, we'll learn how to add and subtract vectors geometrically.
